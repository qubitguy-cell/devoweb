Help & suggestions

Find your way around Daily Bread and tell us what would make it better.

Today

Read the daily reflection and scripture. Mark the reading complete when you have spent time with it.

Prayer room

Use the breathing rhythm, read a prayer prompt, or write a private prayer. Your archive stays in this browser. Each entry can be edited, deleted, or deliberately shared.

Scripture study

Enter a passage such as “John 3:16” to look it up, or search a topic in Daily Bread’s included readings. Recent searches stay on this device.

Community & counseling

Community posts are visible to signed-in members. Counseling requests and replies are private to you and the selected guide. Open a request from Notifications, or choose Counsel & advice then Your conversations to continue later. Scholars may seek counsel from another guide.

Journal library

Browse shared PDFs, or sign in to upload a PDF you have permission to share. You can edit or remove your own journal.

Account & notifications

Edit your profile, photo, email, or scholar listing from Account. Notifications link directly to private counseling rooms; unread counts clear when the inbox or relevant room is opened.

Your feedback

Suggest an update

Report a problem or describe a feature that would help. Suggestions open as GitHub issues for the site maintainers.
